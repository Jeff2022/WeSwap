## Project Overview
The project currently supports swaps between USDC and AVAX on the Avalanche Fuji Testnet. 

Users can convert USDC into AVAX, and they can also exchange AVAX for USDC.

As a decentralized exchanges (DEX), users hold the private keys of their crypto wallets and always have complete control over their digital assets.That means more safety for users and lower risk of platform misconduct.

## Project Target


Build a secure, non-custodial, multi-chain decentralized exchange ecosystem originating from Avalanche. Starting with basic USDC-AVAX swaps on Fuji Testnet, the platform will evolve into a standard AMM DEX with community-driven liquidity provision, then integrate cross-chain routing infrastructure to break blockchain barriers.
